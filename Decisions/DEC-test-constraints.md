# Test constraints

**Decision:** `[decision]`
- Only one Steam account is available. Do not pursue another account, multibox workarounds or Steam bypasses.
- Real two-peer testing is externally blocked and excluded from completion requirements. It is not a blocker.
- Simulated sleepers and one-client ownership transitions are **not** two-peer verification. Label them accurately.
- Sandboxing is unnecessary. Use targeted backups and disposable characters, worlds or copies for destructive tests.
- Do not start the production world merely to tick a box.
